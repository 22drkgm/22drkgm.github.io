% ---------------------------------------------------------- Research
\section*{Research Experience}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{PhD Research --- Aerosol Lab, IIT (ISM) Dhanbad}
    \hfill\mbox{Aug 2022} -- Present

    Year-long field campaign (March 2023 -- March 2024, $N = 131$ paired samples)
    characterising PM\textsubscript{2.5} and PM\textsubscript{10}
    in Dhanbad, designed for source apportionment; the same dataset underpins the
    thermodynamic work. Multiple sampling campaigns run in parallel (ambient and
    primary emission-source sampling). Work has spanned:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textit{Chemical properties:} Water-soluble ionic
        composition (IC), OC/EC (thermal-optical reflectance),
        metals (ICP-AES, ED-XRF)
        \item \textit{Optical properties:} Light absorption and
        source-resolved optical characterisation of carbonaceous
        aerosol
        \item \textit{Isotopic properties:} Carbon isotope
        ($\delta^{13}$C) analysis for source attribution of
        carbonaceous fractions
        \item \textit{Thermodynamic properties:} Aerosol pH, aerosol
        liquid water content (ALWC), and gas--particle partitioning
        using ISORROPIA-II and HETP v1.0; counterfactual simulations
        to quantify non-volatile cation effects on nitrate stability
        \item \textit{Source apportionment:} Emission source
        profiling; PMF and ME-2 receptor modelling (in progress)
        \item \textit{Transport analysis:} Back-trajectory cluster
        analysis using HYSPLIT/TrajStat to link air mass origins
        to aerosol composition
    \end{itemize}

    \item \textbf{Laboratory Setup --- Aerosol Lab, IIT (ISM) Dhanbad}
    \hfill 2022 -- Present

    Designed and commissioned the Aerosol Lab (layout, instrument siting,
    power and UPS load planning, procurement); manage day-to-day operations
    and consumables.
\end{itemize}
