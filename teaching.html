Teaching

Industry training, PG and PhD research-skills sessions, MTech statistics labs, and undergraduate air and noise pollution practicals at IIT (ISM) Dhanbad, 2023–present.

What I taught

Python for data analysis — Management Development Program

Bharat Coking Coal Ltd (BCCL) executives · Feb & Mar 2025 · resource person

Two three-day modules for the same cohort of BCCL executives: a basic module in February and an advanced module in March. I taught both end to end, from loading raw data into pandas through cleaning, exploration and descriptive statistics to correlation and publication-quality plots, worked on the company's own operational data. Honorarium at Assistant Professor rate.

  • pandas
  • data cleaning
  • exploratory analysis
  • descriptive statistics
  • correlation
  • matplotlib
  • seaborn
  • Jupyter

Scientific writing in LaTeX and literature search — Research Methodology

NESC595 / NESC596 · Winter 2024–25 · MTech and PhD students · course PIs Prof. Riya Dutta and Prof. Vithal Hari

TA during Prof. Vithal Hari's part of the course on scientific writing, built around LaTeX with offline compilation rather than Overleaf alone. I took the tutorial on defining and writing the sections of a paper and was the main grader for the LaTeX assignment: 66 submissions (36 MTech, 30 PhD), marked on sectioning, floats, lists, cross-referencing, BibTeX citations, clean compilation and document structure.

I also took a session on literature search for systematic reviews and for students' own research: finding journals and papers in Scopus and Web of Science, building keyword and search strings, filtering and sorting results, and organising them in a literature-review matrix.

  • LaTeX
  • BibTeX
  • Zotero
  • offline TeX compilation
  • Scopus
  • Web of Science
  • search strings
  • systematic literature review

Statistics in Excel — Environmental Computational Lab

NESC511 · Winter 2024–25 and 2025–26 · MTech · under Prof. Saifi Izhar

The lab is split into four tracks (MATLAB, Excel, Python, R); I ran the Excel track. Weekly sessions combined theory with hands-on work. In 2025–26 this was three lectures with labs on correlation and linear regression, descriptive statistics, and hypothesis testing, followed by a data-analysis assignment that graded students on their inferences, not just their numbers.

  • descriptive statistics
  • correlation
  • linear regression
  • hypothesis testing
  • t-tests
  • Excel Analysis ToolPak

Air and noise pollution laboratory — two full undergraduate batches

ESC252 · Monsoon 2024–25 · under Prof. Pandiyan  /  NESC104 · Winter 2024–25 · under Prof. Saifi Izhar · B.Tech 1st and 2nd year

Ran the practicals for both batches: ambient particulate and gaseous sampling, gravimetric and chemical analysis, and noise measurement, with students operating the instruments themselves.
