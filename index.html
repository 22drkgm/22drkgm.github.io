CV (PDF) Email Google Scholar

Research

Coal mines, coke ovens, power plants, brick kilns and traffic release particles into the same shallow boundary layer. What those particles do to health and climate depends on what they are made of and how they age. I study this in Dhanbad, India’s coal capital, one of the most polluted regions of the Indo-Gangetic Plain.

Focus

A year-long PM2.5 and PM10 campaign, examined through its chemical, optical, isotopic and thermodynamic properties: what the particles are made of, where they come from, and how acidic and water-rich they become. I also designed and commissioned the Aerosol Lab at IIT (ISM) Dhanbad.

Methods

Field sampling and laboratory chemistry, aerosol thermodynamic modelling, source apportionment and back-trajectory analysis, with data analysis in Python and R.
