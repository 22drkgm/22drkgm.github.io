About

4th-year PhD
KGM

Mishra is a fourth-year PhD candidate in the Aerosol Lab at IIT (ISM) Dhanbad, supervised by Prof. Saifi Izhar. His work follows one field campaign — a year of paired PM2.5/PM10 sampling in Dhanbad, March 2023 to March 2024, N = 131 — through four layers of aerosol chemistry: water-soluble ionic composition and OC/EC, light-absorption optics, carbon isotopes (δ13C), and thermodynamic partitioning (ISORROPIA-II / HETP v1.0), now feeding into source apportionment (PMF / ME-2) and HYSPLIT back-trajectory clustering.

He also designed and commissioned the Aerosol Lab itself — instrument siting, power and UPS load planning, procurement — and keeps it running. Two first-author Q1 papers so far; he reviews for Atmospheric Environment: X. Full thesis draft due to his supervisor October 2026, defence expected before March 2027.

Research focus

One dataset, read four different ways — plus the modelling that turns composition into source and the transport analysis that turns source into air-mass history.
