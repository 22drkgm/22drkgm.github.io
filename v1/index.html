01 Research

A coal-industrial airshed

Fine particulate matter is a leading environmental health risk in India, and the Indo-Gangetic Plain carries some of the highest PM2.5 burdens in the world.

Coal regions add their own signature: dust from open-cast mining, smoke from coal fires and combustion, and secondary aerosol formed from SO2 and NOx. Separating those contributions needs source profiles measured locally, not borrowed from elsewhere.

My work at the Aerosol Lab, IIT (ISM) Dhanbad, follows PM2.5 and PM10 in Dhanbad, India's coal capital. I designed and commissioned the lab (layout, instrument siting, power and UPS planning, procurement) and run it day to day. I am supervised by Prof. Saifi Izhar.

The core dataset is one year-long field campaign: paired PM2.5/PM10 sampling in Dhanbad from March 2023 to March 2024 (N = 131), run alongside sampling of primary emission sources. I study it through its chemical, optical, isotopic and thermodynamic properties. Aerosol pH, liquid water content and gas–particle partitioning come from ISORROPIA-II and HETP v1.0, cross-checked against E-AIM and MOSAIC's MESA/MTEM, with counterfactual runs that isolate how non-volatile cations control nitrate.

Current work covers thermodynamic modelling of aerosol acidity and nitrate partitioning, source apportionment built on locally measured source profiles, and particle hygroscopicity.

Methods and toolkit

Chemical
Water-soluble ions by ion chromatography (IC), OC/EC by thermal-optical reflectance, metals by ICP-AES and ED-XRF
Optical
Light absorption and source-resolved optical characterisation of carbonaceous aerosol
Isotopic
Carbon isotope (δ13C) analysis for source attribution of carbonaceous fractions
Thermodynamic
Aerosol pH, aerosol liquid water content (ALWC) and gas–particle partitioning with ISORROPIA-II and HETP v1.0; cross-checked with E-AIM and MOSAIC
Source apportionment
Emission source profiling; PMF and ME-2 receptor modelling (in progress)
Transport
Back-trajectory cluster analysis with HYSPLIT and TrajStat
Software
Python (pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, statsmodels, SciPy, matplotlib, seaborn), R (openair, ggplot2), geopandas, QGIS, LaTeX

04 Teaching

Teaching and instruction

Python for data analysis

Management Development Program, Bharat Coking Coal Ltd (BCCL) executives, February and March 2025. Resource person; honorarium at Assistant Professor rate.

Two three-day modules for the same cohort: basic in February, advanced in March. I taught both end to end: loading raw data into pandas, cleaning, exploration and descriptive statistics, correlation, and plotting with matplotlib and seaborn, all on the company's own operational data.

Scientific writing in LaTeX and literature search

Research Methodology (NESC595/NESC596), Winter 2024–25, MTech and PhD students. Course PIs Prof. Riya Dutta and Prof. Vithal Hari.

TA in Prof. Vithal Hari's part of the course on scientific writing, built around LaTeX with offline compilation rather than Overleaf alone. I took the tutorial on defining and writing the sections of a paper and was the main grader for the LaTeX assignment: 66 submissions (36 MTech, 30 PhD), marked on sectioning, floats, lists, cross-referencing, BibTeX citations, clean compilation and structure.

I also took a session on literature search for systematic reviews and for students' own research: finding journals and papers in Scopus and Web of Science, building keyword and search strings, filtering and sorting results, and keeping a literature-review matrix.

Statistics in Excel

Environmental Computational Lab (NESC511), Winter 2024–25 and 2025–26, MTech. Under Prof. Saifi Izhar.

The lab runs four tracks (MATLAB, Excel, Python and R). I ran the Excel track, with theory and hands-on work in each weekly session. In 2025–26 that was three lectures with labs on correlation and linear regression, descriptive statistics, and hypothesis testing, followed by a data-analysis assignment graded on students' inferences, not just their numbers.

Air and noise pollution laboratory

ESC252, Monsoon 2024–25, under Prof. Pandiyan; NESC104, Winter 2024–25, under Prof. Saifi Izhar. B.Tech 1st and 2nd year.

Ran the practicals for two full batches: ambient particulate and gaseous sampling, gravimetric and chemical analysis, and noise measurement, with students operating the instruments themselves.
